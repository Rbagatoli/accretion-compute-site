What it would look like
  • Gas engines on a small parcel near the gas, a battery to smooth the load, and factory-built compute modules leased to an AI company.
  • No grid connection and no export: all of the power is used on site.
  • We build and run the site. The owner puts in no capital.
  • Multi-year gas and land agreements, matched to the tenant's lease, at a fixed price.
Worth checking
  • Latest flow: the landfill is growing, so today's figure may be higher
  • Status of the City's 2024 landfill gas beneficial-use RFP
  • Space for a small fenced parcel near the flare
  • Gas quality for engines
