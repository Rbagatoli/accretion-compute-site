What you get

  • You keep every environmental credit you hold today; new credits the project creates are shared on terms we agree.
  • Revenue on gas the flare now burns for nothing.
  • A land lease for about 7 acres near the flare.
  • No capital from you for the engines or the data center. Your collection system stays as it is; the only compliance changes are the permit updates mapped below.

How the gas is paid for: about $214,000 to $324,000 a year ($2.1 million to $3.2 million over a 10-year term): a land lease of $1,000 to $2,000 an acre a year, plus the gas at $2.00 to $3.00 per MMBtu. The gas price is a floor price plus a share of what the power from the gas is worth, higher for gas that meets the engines' quality spec, set once we've looked at the site together.

Permits we've mapped

  • Air permit for the engines: the TCEQ Electric Generating Unit Standard Permit (30 TAC 116.601-615). The plant also registers with the PUCT (the Public Utility Commission of Texas) before first power (16 TAC 25.109).
  • Under the Governor's directive of September 21, 2026, TCEQ is holding all data center permits, with no end date set. It would cover this project's permits too: planning and agreements can go ahead meanwhile, and the filings would wait for the hold to lift.
  • The landfill's own permits: As we read it, you would update the landfill's gas collection plan and Title V permit to name our engines, with a TCEQ municipal solid waste permit change.
  • Local step: none expected if the parcel is outside city limits. The EPA location is in unincorporated McLennan County, next to the City of Lacy Lakeview, and Texas counties don't zone. Worth confirming

As of November 18, 2026. Lines marked “Worth confirming” are our reading, to check with the agency.

What it would look like

  • Our own gas engines, a battery to smooth the load, and factory-built compute modules on a fenced parcel near the flare.
  • Engines and computers share one 480-volt system on site: no substation, no grid tie.
  • We build and run the site and lease the compute modules to an AI company.
