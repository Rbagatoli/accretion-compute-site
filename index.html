01What we do

Powered capacity for AI

Accretion Compute builds and operates the physical layer AI runs on: power, cooling, space and connectivity. We lease it to AI companies on multi-year terms. Tenants bring their own hardware; we run the site.

  • Power

    Firm, on-site energy sized to the load, with backup designed in from day one.

  • Cooling

    Built for the heat density of modern accelerators, not retrofitted for it.

  • Space

    Secure, purpose-built halls with room to grow alongside the tenant.

  • Connectivity

    Sites chosen with fiber in reach, so compute is close to where it is needed.

02Why energy at the source

The grid is the bottleneck. Energy is not.

New data centres wait years in line to connect to the grid. Meanwhile, energy is already being produced at sites where no one is using it fully.

Accretion places compute next to that energy and runs it behind the meter, on sources such as:

  • Pipeline and gathering-system gas
  • Landfill gas
  • Existing industrial sites with power already in place

That means capacity sooner and energy that would otherwise go unused put to work.

03The name

Accretion

In astrophysics, accretion is matter spiralling in and gathering around a central mass. An accretion disk is what makes quasars shine: one of the most efficient ways known to turn mass into light.

We do the same on the ground: gather scattered, stranded energy and concentrate it into compute.

Accretion Compute is part of Ion Group, alongside Proton Mining.
