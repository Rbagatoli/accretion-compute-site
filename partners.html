Energy and land partners

Turn energy and land into a long-term tenant

If you produce gas, run a landfill or own the right land, AI capacity can be a steady, long-term use for it. We bring the tenant, the build and the operations.

Does your site fit?

Who we work with

Partners at the source

Gas producers

Operators with firm volumes and pipeline or gathering-system access looking for a long-term local buyer.

Landfill operators

Sites with steady landfill gas that could generate power on site.

Landowners

Land near energy and fiber, with room for a site to be built and to grow.

Industrial sites

Existing facilities with power, permits or infrastructure that could be put to new use.

What you get

A steady use for what you have

  • A long-term buyer for gas, on terms that match a multi-year lease
  • Lease income for land that may be earning little today
  • A use for existing infrastructure such as pipelines, roads and pads
  • An operator who builds, staffs and runs the site

What we look for

The right starting point

  • Firm gas that can last for many years
  • Pipeline or gathering access for a backup supply
  • Land with room to build and expand
  • Fiber within reach, and year-round road access

How it starts

Three steps, no obligation

  1. Introduction

    A short call about your site: what energy and land you have, and where it is.

  2. Site review

    We review the site with you, under a confidentiality agreement where needed, and tell you plainly if it fits.

  3. Agreement

    If it fits, we agree terms for fuel supply, land or both, aligned with a tenant's lease.
