The model

Everything around the rack

Accretion is a capacity provider, not a cloud. We do not sell GPU-hours or compete with our tenants. We build and operate the site; you own and run your workloads.

  • Power delivered to your racks from on-site generation
  • Cooling designed for your density, air or liquid
  • Space in secure halls with controlled physical access
  • Connectivity through fiber routes and cross-connects
  • Operations: on-site staff and remote hands

Anatomy of a site

One site, from fuel to fiber

Energy enters on one side. Compute leaves on the other. Everything in between is built and run by Accretion.

Questions

Capacity FAQ

Is capacity available today?

Not yet. Accretion Compute is in development, and we are matching early tenants with sites now. If you are planning capacity, talk to us about your timeline.

Do you own or rent out GPUs?

No. Tenants bring and own their hardware. We provide the energy, building, power, cooling, connectivity and on-site operations around it.

Where will your sites be?

We are evaluating sites in Canada and the United States where firm energy, fiber and land come together. See how we choose sites.

What uptime and redundancy will you offer?

Each site's design, redundancy and service levels are set out in its contract before you commit. We do not publish figures for sites that are not yet built.

Can you support liquid cooling?

Yes. Halls are designed to the tenant's specification, including liquid cooling for high-density accelerators.
