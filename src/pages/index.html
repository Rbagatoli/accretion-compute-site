What we do

Powered capacity for AI

Accretion Compute builds and operates the physical layer AI runs on, and leases it to AI companies on multi-year terms. Tenants bring their own hardware. We bring the energy, the building and the people who run it.

Space

Why energy at the source

The grid is the bottleneck. Energy is not.

New data centres can wait years for a grid connection. Meanwhile, energy is already being produced at sites where no one is using all of it.

Building at the source skips the longest line in the industry. It also puts gas that is stranded, flared or under-used to productive work.
