Who we work with

Partners at the source

Four kinds of partner. Pick yours to see what we look for and how a deal usually works.

Gas producers

A long-term local buyer for gas, close to where it is produced. We build generation next to your supply and buy gas on terms matched to the tenant's lease.

  • Firm volumes that can last for many years
  • Pipeline or gathering access for a backup supply
  • Land near the supply, or a landowner we can work with

Usually a gas supply agreement, sometimes with a land lease.

Landfill operators

A steady use for landfill gas that may be flared today. We build generation on or next to the landfill and take the gas for the length of the tenant's lease.

  • An established gas collection system
  • Gas flow records we can forecast from
  • Space on or near the landfill to build

Usually a gas agreement with a site lease.

Landowners

Lease income for land that may be earning little today. The best land sits close to gas and fiber, with room for a site to be built and to grow.

  • Gas supply on or near the land
  • Fiber within reach, and year-round road access
  • Enough flat, buildable ground to expand

Usually a long-term land lease. See our guide to leasing land to a data center.

Industrial sites

A new use for a site with power, permits or infrastructure already in place, whether it is running, idle or winding down. Reusing what is there can save years.

  • Electrical infrastructure or gas on site
  • Land zoned for industrial use
  • Buildings, pads or roads that can be reused

Usually a site lease, or an agreement to share infrastructure.

The path to first power

From first call to a running site

Six stages. Only the first three need much of your time, and you can stop at any point before an agreement is signed.

A short call about your site

Your part: a conversation

Tell us what you have and where it is: the gas, the land, the access. Rough numbers are fine. You can start with the site check or the form instead of a call.

  • No obligation and no cost to talk
  • A straight answer on whether it is worth a closer look
  • Or start online: site check or submit a site

We review the site with you

Your part: share what you know

We look at the energy, land, fiber, access and permits, under a confidentiality agreement where needed. Then we tell you plainly whether the site fits, and why.

  • Gas volumes, quality and how long they last
  • Land, access, fiber and nearby infrastructure
  • Local permits and approvals

Terms that match the tenant's lease

Your part: agree terms

If the site fits, we agree terms for gas supply, land or both. Their length is matched to the lease our tenant signs, so the whole chain holds together.

  • Gas supply agreement, land lease, or both
  • Length matched to the tenant's lease
  • Room to expand written in where it makes sense

Design and permits

Accretion runs it

We design the site around the energy and the tenant's needs, and work through the permits and approvals it needs.

  • Site layout, generation and cooling design
  • Permits and local approvals
  • We keep you updated at each step

We build the site

Accretion runs it

Generation, power, cooling and the AI hall go up on the site. You keep running your own operations alongside.

  • Generation, power and cooling installed
  • The hall built to the tenant's design
  • Tested and commissioned before the tenant moves in

A running site, for years

Accretion runs it

The tenant runs its workloads. We run the site day to day. You supply the gas or the land, and are paid on the agreed terms.

  • On-site staff and maintenance
  • A steady, long-term use for your gas or land
  • Room to grow the site together

Questions

Partner FAQ

Does it cost anything to find out if my site fits?

No. The first call, the site check and the site review carry no obligation. You can stop at any point before an agreement is signed.

Do I have to sell my land?

No. Most partnerships are a lease or a supply agreement, and you keep ownership. The structure is agreed for each site.

Will my information be kept private?

Yes. We review sites under a confidentiality agreement where needed. We review sites together with our energy partner, so they may see it too. See our privacy notice.

How long do agreements run?

They are matched to the lease our AI tenant signs, which typically runs for several years. The exact term is agreed for each site.

What if my gas output falls over time?

We look for supply that can last for many years, and design in a backup supply path where we can. If your gas is short-lived or interruptible, Bitcoin mining may be a better fit (see below).

Do I need to run anything at the site?

No. You keep producing or collecting the gas, or providing the land. We build, staff and run the site.

Where are you looking for sites?

In Canada and the United States, wherever firm energy, fiber and land come together. See how we choose sites.

Flare or declining gas?

Interruptible or short-lived gas is a better fit for Bitcoin mining. Our sister company Proton Mining specialises in it.
