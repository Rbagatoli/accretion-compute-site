About

Building compute where energy gathers

Accretion Compute is an Ion Group company. We are building AI capacity at the source of energy, and we are at the start of that work.

The name

Why "Accretion"

In astrophysics, accretion is how matter gathers around a source of gravity, forming a disk that becomes one of the brightest things in the universe.

That is the idea behind the company: compute gathers around a source of energy. Our mark is drawn from an accretion disk.
