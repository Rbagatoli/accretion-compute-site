What it would look like
  • Factory-built compute modules on a fenced parcel at or near Dallman, leased to an AI company, with a battery and backup engines for outages.
  • Firm power from CWLP under a long-term service agreement, at a negotiated rate that covers the full cost of service, so other customers carry none of it. No export.
  • We build and run the site. The owner puts in no capital.
  • Multi-year power and land agreements, matched to the tenant's lease.
Worth checking
  • The City Council's decision on the 2018 high-load rate, and a negotiated rate for a new large load
  • MISO surplus interconnection service at Unit 4's point of interconnection
  • Illinois EPA Bureau of Air construction permit for the backup engines
  • City of Springfield zoning review for a data center use
  • Land at or near Dallman that CWLP would lease
