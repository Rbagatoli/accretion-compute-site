What it would look like
  • Gas engines on a small parcel near the gas, a battery to smooth the load, and factory-built compute modules leased to an AI company.
  • No grid connection and no export: all of the power is used on site.
  • We build and run the site. The owner puts in no capital.
  • Multi-year gas and land agreements, matched to the tenant's lease, at a fixed price.
Worth checking
  • Daily flow and methane by well for 12 to 24 months
  • Engines as qualifying destruction devices under the ARB Mine Methane Capture protocol; the credits stay with the project
  • PA DEP air and oil and gas approvals at Mine 84; Illinois EPA air permits at Crown I and Green Valley
  • Gas rights holder at each site

Source: Climate Action Reserve project reports, 2026 (Mine 84 to March 31, Crown I to April 30, Green Valley to August 31): about 6.3 MMcf/d of mine gas at about 79% methane, about 4,966 Mcf/d of methane in all.
