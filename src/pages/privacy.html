Privacy

Privacy notice

Short version: this site sets no cookies, does not follow you across other sites and keeps only what you send us through a form. We count visits in aggregate only.

What this site collects

Very little. There are no cookies and no advertising trackers on this website.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits: which pages are viewed, the website that linked you here, and your rough country, browser and device type. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not follow you across other websites. We only see totals, not individual visitors.

The contact and site forms

When you press the button on the contact form or the submit-a-site form, what you typed is sent to us and kept in a database on Cloudflare, the company that hosts this site, so we can read it and reply. The page then shows a reference number for your message.

With it we keep the date and time, your rough country (worked out from the connection, not your exact location), which page you first opened on this site and which website linked you here, if any. That last part helps us see which pages are useful. Your browser keeps it only for the current tab and forgets it when the tab closes. We do not keep your IP address. To limit spam, a scrambled form of it is held for one hour and then deleted.

If sending fails, the page offers to open the same message in your own email app instead. Nothing is sent that way until you send that email yourself.

Whether you use a form or email us, we use your message and contact details only to reply and to follow up on what you asked about. We do not sell them. To have a message deleted, write to the address below.

If you send us a site, we review it together with our energy partner, Proton Mining, so they may see the site details and your contact details. We use them only to review that site and to contact you about it.

Services this site uses

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service.
  • Visit counts. Cloudflare Web Analytics, described above, runs a small script on each page. Cloudflare's own privacy policy covers how it handles that data.
  • Fonts. Text fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads.

Questions

Write to contact@accretioncompute.com with any question about this notice or about information you have sent us.
