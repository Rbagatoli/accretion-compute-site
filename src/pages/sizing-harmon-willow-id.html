What it would look like
  • Gas engines on a small parcel near the gas, a battery to smooth the load, and factory-built compute modules leased to an AI company.
  • No grid connection and no export: all of the power is used on site.
  • We build and run the site. The owner puts in no capital.
  • Multi-year gas and land agreements, matched to the tenant's lease, at a fixed price.
Worth checking
  • Built in blocks: about 1 to 1.5 MMcf/d first, more of the field as it comes back
  • Fixed price for five years at the tailgate, a floor under pipeline prices
  • Idaho DEQ permit to construct (IDAPA 58.01.01) and a Payette County conditional use permit
  • Dry cooling under Idaho H0895

Source: Idaho Oil and Gas Conservation Commission filings: the fields averaged about 4 MMcf/d from July 2025 to June 2026, and four wells are back at about 6 MMcf/d (Q2 2026 Commission Report).
