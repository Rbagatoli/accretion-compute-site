What it would look like
  • Gas engines on a small parcel near the gas, a battery to smooth the load, and factory-built compute modules leased to an AI company.
  • No grid connection and no export: all of the power is used on site.
  • We build and run the site. The owner puts in no capital.
  • Multi-year gas and land agreements, matched to the tenant's lease, at a fixed or floored price.
Worth checking
  • Who holds the gas rights since the 2020 engine shutdown
  • Whether the old 12 kV point and pad can be reused
  • Latest flow and methane data, and the outlook to closure (2063)
  • Rushcreek Township zoning for the parcel
  • The old engine plant ran 4.8 MW; engine choice sets the final figure
