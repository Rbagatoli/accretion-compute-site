What it would look like
  • Our own gas engines on a fenced parcel near the gas, a battery to smooth the load, and factory-built compute modules leased to an AI company.
  • No grid connection and no export: all of the power is used on site.
  • We build and run the site. The owner puts in no capital.
  • Multi-year gas and land agreements, matched to the tenant's lease, at a fixed price.
Worth checking
  • Gas rights and any existing gas agreement
  • Michigan EGLE permit to install (Rule 336.1201)
  • Tie-in under the landfill's Title V permit (NSPS Subpart XXX, NESHAP Subpart AAAA)
  • County or municipal zoning for the data hall

Source: EPA Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program, 2023: about 3,670 scfm collected at 49.6% methane. This is an estimate from public data, not an offer: no cost and no obligation. The real price and terms are set in the gas and land agreements after a site review.
