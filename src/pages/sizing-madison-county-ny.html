What the County gets

  • The County keeps every environmental credit it holds today; new credits the project creates are shared on terms we agree.
  • Revenue on gas the flare now burns for nothing.
  • A land lease for about 6 acres near the flare.
  • No capital from the County for the engines or the data center. The County's collection system stays as it is; the only compliance changes are the permit updates mapped below.

How the gas is paid for: about $181,000 to $274,000 a year ($1.8 million to $2.7 million over a 10-year term): a land lease of $1,000 to $2,000 an acre a year, plus the gas at $2.00 to $3.00 per MMBtu. The gas price is a floor price plus a share of what the power from the gas is worth, higher for gas at the County's target quality, set once we've looked at the site together.

Permits we've mapped

  • Air permit for the engines: a NYSDEC Air State Facility permit (6 NYCRR 201-5) with a 201-7 cap, a limit written into the permit that keeps the plant under major-source levels. Under the Uniform Procedures Act, New York's permit-review law, the cap makes it a “major project” review, with a decision due in 90 days. Worth confirming
  • Local step: a Town of Lincoln Planning Board special permit in the I-C zoning district, with site plan review and SEQR (the state environmental review), and a Town Board special use permit for the battery that smooths the load on the engines (Tier 3 under Local Law 1 of 2024, at 500 kWh or more).
  • The landfill's own permit: a tie-in with the County's Title V permit for the landfill. The lease would follow County Law 215 or General Municipal Law 120-w. Worth confirming
  • No data center moratorium found in the Town of Lincoln (as of November 17, 2026).

As of November 18, 2026. Lines marked “Worth confirming” are our reading, to check with the agency.

What it would look like

  • Our own gas engines, a battery to smooth the load, and factory-built compute modules on a fenced parcel near the flare.
  • Engines and computers share one 480-volt system on site: no substation, no grid tie.
  • We build and run the site and lease the compute modules to an AI company.
