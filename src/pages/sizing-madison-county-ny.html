What it would look like
  • Gas engines, a battery to smooth the load, and factory-built compute modules leased to an AI company, on a small parcel near the gas.
  • The County keeps running the wellfield and delivers gas to our meter. Gas at the target quality is paid at a higher price.
  • Runs on its own engines. If the earlier project's grid connection is still in place, it could serve as backup.
  • We build and run the site; the County puts in no capital. Engine heat is available for the ARE Park.
To firm up the numbers
  • 12 to 24 months of flow, methane and oxygen readings
  • Equipment still in place from the earlier project
  • Whether the earlier National Grid connection is still in place, and its size
  • Utilities and acres at the ARE Park; gas quality (siloxanes, H2S)

Source: Madison County gas readings shared in November 2026: today about 350 scfm at 30% methane; the County's target is 350 to 375 scfm at 45 to 50% methane (the target column uses 360 scfm at 48%). These are estimates from the County's readings, not an offer: no cost and no obligation. Twelve to 24 months of readings will firm them up; the real price and terms are set in the gas and land agreements.
