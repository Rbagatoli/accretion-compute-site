    How the site works

    From your gas to computing, all inside one fence

    GAS

    Your supply

    A metered tap at your plant, gathering line or landfill header.

    CONDITION

    Gas treatment

    Moisture, siloxane and sulfur removed so the engines run clean.

    GENERATE

    Gas engines

    Modular generator sets with a spare unit, sized to your gas.

    STEADY

    Battery

    Absorbs the fast swings in AI load so the engines see a steady demand.

    COMPUTE

    Compute modules

    Factory-built, liquid-cooled AI modules, added about 1 MW at a time.

    COOL

    Closed-loop cooling

    Dry coolers in a sealed loop: no evaporative water use by design.

    What happens next

    From first email to first power

    1. STEP 1

      Data

      You share gas flow and quality, and a site plan. We confirm the size.

    2. STEP 2

      Letter of intent

      Non-binding terms for the gas and an option on the land.

    3. STEP 3

      Studies

      Gas analysis, geotechnical, air permit and local approvals, at our cost.

    4. STEP 4

      Agreements

      Gas purchase agreement and ground lease signed.

    5. STEP 5

      Build

      Power plant and compute modules installed; we operate the site.
