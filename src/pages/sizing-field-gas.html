The math

Gas at 1,037 Btu per cubic foot; engines at a heat rate of 9,000 Btu/kWh; compute is power divided by 1.2 for cooling and losses; compute modules of about 1 MW each; larger sites use larger engines; land of 5 acres plus 1.2 acres per MW; 95% run time. Prices in US dollars.

What it would look like
  • Engines and compute modules on a small parcel next to the tap, with no grid connection.
  • Steady take, every day, for the length of the tenant's lease. We build and run the site; the owner puts in no capital.
  • Needs gas that can be contracted for years, ideally with a gathering-line backup.

This is a first read, not an offer: no cost and no obligation. The real price, volume and term are set in the gas agreement after a review of the supply, the surface and the route to fiber. Send a field or tap location and a flow, and we reply with a sizing for that point.
