What it would look like
  • Factory-built compute modules on a fenced parcel at or next to Plant #1, leased to an AI company, with a battery and backup engines for outages.
  • Firm power from MPW under a long-term service agreement, at a rate that covers the full cost of service, so other customers carry none of it. No export.
  • We build and run the site. The owner puts in no capital.
  • Multi-year power and land agreements, matched to the tenant's lease.
Worth checking
  • A first phase of about 5 MW (5 modules), growing toward 18 MW
  • Which unit retired in 2024, and whether MPW still holds its MISO interconnection rights
  • Iowa DNR Air Quality Bureau construction permit, or a permit exemption, for the backup engines
  • City of Muscatine zoning review for a data center use at or next to Plant #1 (Muscatine County's 2025 ordinance covers land outside the city)
  • A large-load rate from MPW and a land lease at or next to the plant
