Privacy notice

Short version: this site does not track you and does not store anything you type.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. There are no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers on this website.

The contact form

The contact form does not send data to us or to any server. When you press the button, it opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent until you send that email yourself.

If you email us, we use your message and contact details only to reply and to follow up on what you asked about. We do not sell or share them.

Services this site uses

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service.
  • Fonts. Text fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads.

Questions

Write to hello@accretioncompute.com with any question about this notice or about information you have sent us.
